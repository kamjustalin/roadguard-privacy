Privacy Policy for RoadGuard SOS

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Contact: stalinkamju@gmail.com

Developer: Stalin Kamju (Stansoft.in)

RoadGuard SOS ("the app") is a road-safety tool that lets you record video and, only when you choose to, notify emergency contacts by text, share the video, share your live location, or call for help. This policy explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

What the app never does

  • It never sends a text message, places a phone call, or shares anything without you tapping to confirm inside your phone's own Messages, Share, or Phone screen. The app cannot see or read your other messages or call history.
  • It does not show ads and does not use any analytics or advertising SDK.
  • It does not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Data stored only on your device

The following stays in the app's local storage on your phone and is never sent to us or any server:

  • Emergency contacts you add (name, phone number, relationship) — stored on-device, deleted if you remove the contact or uninstall the app.
  • Your alert message template and settings.
  • Recorded videos — saved to the app's private storage (and to your photo library only if you tap "Save a copy to gallery"). Videos are only transmitted off your device when you use Share or Message, and then only to the destination you pick.

Live location sharing

If you tap Start sharing my location, your device's coordinates are sent approximately every 15 seconds to our Firebase (Google Cloud) project so the link you share can show your live position on a map to whoever holds that link — the same trust model as WhatsApp's or Google Maps' "share live location." This continues only while RoadGuard SOS is open in the foreground; it does not track you in the background or after you tap Stop sharing.

  • The link is a long random ID that is not guessable in practice, but anyone who has the link can view that session's location until it is stopped or expires (sessions auto-expire after up to 2 hours).
  • Location pings for a session are automatically deleted approximately 30 minutes after the session ends or expires — whether you tap Stop sharing yourself or simply close the app.
  • This data is processed by Firebase (a Google Cloud service) — see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • You can delete any emergency contact, or all of them, at any time in the app.
  • You can stop a live location share at any time; the link stops updating immediately.
  • You can revoke Camera, Microphone, Location, or Contacts permission at any time in your device's system Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (contacts, settings, cached videos).

Children's privacy

RoadGuard SOS is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age defined by your local law) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change to reflect it.
